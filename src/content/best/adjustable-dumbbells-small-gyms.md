 ## Questions to ask before buying

 - Will this gear fit your available workout and storage space?
 - Can you find a trustworthy manual, stated warranty and clear return terms?
 - Are consumables, repairs or replacement parts likely to add ongoing costs?
 - Does the chosen product fit your experience level and intended exercises?

 ## Mistakes to avoid

 - Buying beyond your storage capacity.
- Ignoring adjustment time.
- Skipping warranty and replacement parts research.
