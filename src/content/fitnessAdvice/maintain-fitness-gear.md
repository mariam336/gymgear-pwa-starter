## Start with the product manual

Check the manufacturer's instructions before cleaning, repairing or storing any equipment. Materials and safe care methods vary, so one universal method is not appropriate for every model.

## Make your routine practical

- Check the available floor space before bringing new equipment home.
- Store items in a way that avoids unnecessary obstacles and damage.
- Inspect signs of wear and follow any relevant replacement guidance.
- Keep written notes on maintenance, warranties and product models.

## What to avoid

Avoid unsupported modifications, incompatible cleaners and storage methods that conflict with the supplied manual. Be cautious of promotional claims about performance or medical benefits.

## Practical next step

Identify one piece of equipment you currently own, find its manual, and create a simple care checklist from the documented instructions.
