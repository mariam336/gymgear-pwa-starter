## The short answer

Neither equipment type is universally better. The sensible choice depends on available space, intended use, maintenance, and what is actually verified for a specific model.

## Who should consider folding treadmills?

Consider this option if its strengths align with the space, activity, and convenience requirements you have identified. Compare the exact product before buying.

## Who should consider non-folding treadmills?

Consider this choice when its trade-offs suit your routine better. Model-specific stability, upkeep and warranty matter more than the category label alone.

## What evidence would settle the decision?

Compare documented measurements and, where possible, independent test observations for specific models. Do not assume one category always wins on safety, durability or value.
